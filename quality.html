Documentation quality and technical debt¶

Google-style docstrings use descriptions, typed parameters, return values, errors and runnable examples. Napoleon converts them to reStructuredText for autodoc. Examples use one domain schema, with synthetic local data and no provider calls. -W makes warnings fail the build; -n checks unresolved references. A separate doctest build executes examples.

chinchilla_quality_audit.md records six operational quality checks and the limits of their attribution. The supplied handout cites Chinchilla Chapter 7 but does not enumerate its six principles. The textbook passage was not provided; this package does not invent its exact wording or claim a page-level reading. Replace the operational labels with verified course labels if needed.

technical_debt_audit.md records a token-based scan of authored Python comments. Vendored assets and generated output are excluded. Unresolved markers must reference a real GitLab issue ID; fabricated IDs are not accepted.
